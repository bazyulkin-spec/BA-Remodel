## Full description
BA-Remodel helps you plan a tile layout before the first square metre is bought.

Draw the room — rectangular, L-shaped or with any number of corners — and add cutouts for
columns and boxing. Set tile size and grout width, pick a pattern: grid, offset, herringbone,
any rotation. The plan updates live, and you can drag the pattern with a finger to find the
layout with the least waste.

DECOR AND SYMMETRY
• Load a photo of your tile and outline the artwork with a frame
• The app shifts the layout so the artwork lands exactly in the centre of the room
• Decor never falls on a cut tile
• Single insert, 2x2 panel or every third tile

HONEST ESTIMATE
• Area, perimeter, full tiles and cut tiles counted separately
• Cuts per wall: how much to trim and whether both sides match
• Warnings for narrow strips, out-of-square walls and asymmetric layouts
• Reserve of 5, 10 or 15 % and the final amount to buy in pieces and square metres

LAYOUT SUGGESTIONS
The app compares layout options and shows the three best by tile usage and number of cuts,
with the difference against your current choice.

REPORT
A project summary and a PDF to share with the client or take to the shop: parameters,
materials and the cut list.

Projects are stored on your device. No internet required. Russian and English interface.

Estimates only: verify dimensions on site and keep a reserve for breakage and cuts.
